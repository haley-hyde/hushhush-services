These people are active. They do good work. They know what they're doing.

We don't sell this around follower counts. For most of the businesses we work with (contractors, architects, restaurants, local service and professional work) social media isn't a growth channel so much as it's proof. Someone hears about you through a referral, looks you up, and decides in about ten seconds whether they trust you.

A consistent, well-organized presence is what tips that decision. It says the business is active, the work is real, and someone's paying attention. That's the outcome we're building toward: not virality, not a follower number, not a guaranteed lead count. A body of work someone can look at and trust.

clarifications

A few things worth knowing up front.

01Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. Engagements start with a 30-day initial period so we can both see how the workflow works in practice before talking about anything longer.

02What if I need something outside my package?+

One-off needs get quoted separately. If it turns into a pattern rather than a one-time thing, we'll talk about moving you into a different level of service instead of quietly stacking extra work onto the existing agreement.

03Do you guarantee follower growth?+

No, and I'd be skeptical of anyone who does. Performance depends on far more than posting consistency. What I can build is a presence that's consistent, credible, and useful to someone deciding whether to trust you, which matters more than a follower count anyway.

04Do I need to provide content?+

Depends on the tier. Coordination assumes you're handing over finished material. Shaping assumes you're handing over raw material and we do the shaping. Partnership includes us coming to you to capture some of that material ourselves.

05Can you manage my advertising?+

Monitoring how your ads are performing and actively managing them are different jobs. Reporting is folded into Shaping and Partnership; full campaign management is scoped and priced separately once we know what you're trying to do.

06Can you come photograph our business or projects?+

Yes. On-site production is available on its own, or built into a Partnership engagement as a standing monthly session.

07What's Metricool, and do I need my own account?+

It's the scheduling, inbox, and analytics infrastructure Hush Hush runs on. Most clients are simply added as a managed brand under the Hush Hush account, no extra subscription required. If you'd rather keep your own account, that works too.

Your marketing problem and your marketing budget are two different things.

Most of the businesses we talk to don't need more marketing. They need marketing that fits the size of the problem they actually have, not the size of the industry that sells to them.

So Hush Hush doesn't start with a package built to look impressive. It starts with the smallest useful scope: the level of involvement that solves what's actually going on. If your content is good but nobody's organizing and publishing it, that's a small problem with a small solution. If you don't have the time or eye to turn what's happening in your business into something worth posting, that's a different, larger job, and it costs more because more of it is now ours to do.

Start with the fundamentals. Get the system working. Then invest further once the work itself shows you where more support would actually help, not before.
